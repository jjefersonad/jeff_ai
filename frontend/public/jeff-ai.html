Jeff AI

Jeff AI is a self-hosted artificial intelligence assistant that you use in the browser. Jeff AI writes and reviews code, researches information, creates documents and spreadsheets, generates images, and organizes email.

What Jeff AI does

Jeff AI is a self-hosted AI assistant. You describe a task in natural language and Jeff AI executes it: editing project files, running tests, using git (with your approval before changes), producing documents and spreadsheets, generating images, searching the web, and managing email. Jeff AI runs on the operator's own server.

Why Jeff AI requests Google user data

If you connect a Gmail account, Jeff AI requests Google OAuth access to your mailbox solely to synchronize incoming email and to send messages on your behalf, limited to the scopes you grant on the consent screen. Access tokens are stored encrypted. You can revoke access at any time from Jeff AI or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
